🫀 Cardio AI — AI-Powered Cardiac Care Platform

An end-to-end, AI-powered cardiac care platform bringing prevention, diagnosis and treatment planning into one intelligent system. It combines 7 specialised AI agents (ECG, echocardiography, lab, imaging, diagnosis, treatment, prognosis), a Master Orchestrator, IoMT medical-device monitoring, and PACS/DICOM imaging analysis.

At its core are 5 clinical risk-prediction models — Heart Attack (AMI), Stroke, Heart Failure, Sudden Cardiac Death and Atrial Fibrillation — each with a C-statistic ≥ 0.75, aligned with AHA guidelines.

♟️ ROMO — Chess Web App (built from scratch)

A fully-playable chess game on the web — with the entire chess engine written from scratch, no game libraries. It handles every rule: legal move validation, check & checkmate, stalemate, castling, en passant and pawn promotion.

Correctness was verified with a standard perft test (move counts matched exactly: 20 / 400 / 8,902). It ships with a built-in AI opponent and a two-player mode — a clear demonstration of clean logic and reliable, well-tested code.
